{# Cookie notice: matches what the site actually uses (services/web.py, static/js/chat.js). #}

PHOENIXMARKET uses a single cookie, which the site needs to work. No advertising, marketing or third-party tracking cookies are used, so no cookie consent is requested.

Essential session cookie

Name: session · Set by: this site · Lifetime: until the browser is closed

Keeps your cart, chosen language and currency, whether you are signed in, and the security token that protects forms against forged requests. It is signed, so it cannot be altered, and scripts on the page cannot read it (HttpOnly).

Site statistics

Visit statistics are kept without cookies: your IP address is not stored, an anonymous id that changes every day is used, and records are deleted after 90 days. Bots and automated tools are not counted.

Browser storage

If you use the support chat, your chat history is kept only in your own browser (localStorage) so it survives moving between pages; the history is not sent to our server, only the message you are sending for an answer. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. If the essential cookie is blocked, the cart and signing in do not work.
