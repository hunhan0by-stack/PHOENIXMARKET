4. Performance and delivery

  • Performance starts once the seller has confirmed the payment. The order status can be followed through the private tracking link given with the order confirmation.
  • Performance is completed within 30 days of the order at the latest.
  • If the seller finds it cannot perform the service, it informs the buyer and refunds the amount paid within 14 days of that notice at the latest.

5. Right of withdrawal

As a rule the buyer may withdraw from the agreement within 14 days without giving a reason. However, under Article 15 of the Distance Contracts Regulation there is no right of withdrawal for:

  • services performed instantly online and intangible goods delivered instantly (Art. 15/1-ğ),
  • services whose performance started, with the consumer's approval, before the withdrawal period ended (Art. 15/1-h).

By ticking the box on the order form the buyer agrees that performance starts right after the payment is confirmed and confirms being informed that the right of withdrawal ends once performance has started. Orders not yet started can be cancelled free of charge from the tracking page before payment.

6. Defective performance

If the service is performed incompletely or defectively, the buyer may, under Law No. 6502, ask for it to be performed again, for a price reduction or to withdraw from the agreement. Requests go to {{ seller.email or missing }} or through the contact form, quoting the order number.

7. Disputes

Disputes arising from this agreement are handled, within the monetary limits set each year by the Ministry of Trade, by the consumer arbitration committees at the buyer's or seller's place of residence, and above those limits by the consumer courts.

8. Entry into force

Before confirming the order, the buyer declares by ticking the box that they have read and accept this text. The time of acceptance and the version of the text are recorded with the order. The agreement is concluded when the order is placed.
