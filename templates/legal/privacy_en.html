2. Data we process

  • Identity and contact: full name, email address.
  • Order data: products and packages bought, amounts, discount code, order notes (e.g. a username you give us for delivery), order status and payment reference.
  • Account data: if you create an account, your email address and an irreversible hash of your password (the password itself is never stored), your favourites and back-in-stock requests.
  • Support messages: messages sent through the contact form and our replies.
  • Technical data: one cookie required to keep your session. Your IP address is not stored; site statistics use an anonymous id that changes every day and are deleted after 90 days.

3. Purposes and legal grounds

  • Taking and fulfilling orders, sending you notifications and giving support: conclusion and performance of a contract (KVKK Art. 5/2-c).
  • Keeping invoices, accounting and other statutory records: compliance with a legal obligation (Art. 5/2-ç).
  • Security of the site, preventing abuse and fraud, improving the service: legitimate interest (Art. 5/2-f).
  • Extra services you request, such as back-in-stock emails: performance of a contract at your request (Art. 5/2-c).

4. Transfers

  • Payment: payments are made on itemsatis. Information you give itemsatis while paying is covered by itemsatis's own privacy notice. We do not transfer data about you to itemsatis; you pass your order number to the seller so the payment can be matched.
  • Hosting and email: the site runs with a hosting provider whose servers are abroad (USA), and order and notification emails are sent through an email service provider. These transfers are made in line with Art. 9 KVKK.

  • To public authorities legally entitled to request it, to the extent the law requires.

5. Retention

  • Order and accounting records: for the statutory retention periods.
  • Account data: while your account is open. You can delete the account yourself on the Account page; data we are not legally required to keep is deleted at once, orders are kept without a link to the account.
  • Support messages: for a reasonable time after your request is resolved and as long as needed for possible disputes.
  • Site statistics: 90 days. Password reset links expire after a short time.

6. Your rights (KVKK Art. 11)

You can ask the data controller to:

  • tell you whether your personal data is processed and, if so, give you information about it,
  • tell you the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly,
  • name the third parties in Turkey or abroad it is transferred to,
  • correct incomplete or inaccurate data, delete or destroy it under the conditions of Art. 7 KVKK, and inform the third parties it was transferred to,
  • object to a result against you arising solely from automated analysis,
  • compensate damage caused by unlawful processing.

If you have an account, Account → Download my data gives you a copy of your data right away. Send other requests to {{ seller.email or missing }} or through the contact form. Requests are answered free of charge within 30 days at the latest.
